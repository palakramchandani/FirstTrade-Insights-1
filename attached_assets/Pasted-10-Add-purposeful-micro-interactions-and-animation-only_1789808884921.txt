10. Add purposeful micro-interactions and animation only after flow logic is fixed.

Use Framer Motion or equivalent. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. All motion must be 180–450ms with ease-out easing.

COHORTS:
- On page load, reveal the four-stage activation journey left to right once.
- Draw the connector line over 450ms.
- Hovering a cohort gently lifts its surface by 2px and reveals the “View customers” action.
- Clicking View customers animates the active cohort filter into the Decisions page.

DECISIONS:
- On customer-row hover, show a subtle background shift and View Decision affordance.
- On row selection, keep the queue stable and slide/fade the detail panel in from the right over 220ms.
- When recommendation changes, fade the old action down and fade the new action up.
- Expand “View score calculation” with a smooth height transition.
- Animate only the five signal bars once when the calculation is opened.

SIMULATOR:
- When a PM changes a product-state control, highlight the changed field briefly in pale blue.
- Recalculate candidate scores immediately.
- Highlight only scores that changed.
- Show “Recommendation updated” for 1.5 seconds.
- Cross-fade Nudge Studio previews when the recommendation changes.
- Never animate all sliders or numbers continuously.

NUDGE STUDIO:
- Make In-app, Push, and WhatsApp real tabs with an animated active underline.
- Cross-fade between channel previews.
- When a PM selects an AI variant, show a small selected check state.
- When the PM edits copy, show “Edited by PM” and enable Save Draft.
- On Save Draft, show a small “Draft saved locally” confirmation.
- On Approve Workflow, transition the workflow from Draft to Approved with a restrained checkmark animation.

POLICY STUDIO:
- Changing a weight or threshold immediately marks the version “Unsaved changes.”
- Animate before/after customer-action changes when Preview Affected Customers is clicked.
- Highlight action changes with blue; removed recommendations with muted grey.
- On Publish Simulated Version, show a policy-version transition:
  Draft v1.1 → Live simulated v1.1
- Do not use confetti.

MEASUREMENT:
- Animate the Control and Treatment journey lines toward the same A2T outcome once when the page opens.
- Reveal event trail steps sequentially on scroll.
- Do not animate charts or show fake performance changes.

GLOBAL:
- Buttons: 150ms hover/press state.
- Inputs: soft focus ring and immediate value feedback.
- Tooltips: 150ms fade for score definitions and compliance notes.
- No loops, floating objects, bouncing, spinning, neon glow, animated backgrounds, or decorative motion.
